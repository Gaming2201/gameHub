Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

Welcome to Game Hub. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of personal information that is collected and recorded by Game Hub and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

Game Hub does not require users to create an account or submit personal information such as names or passwords to play games. However, we may collect non-personal data automatically:

  • Log Files: Like many other websites, we use log files that record visitors when they visit the site. This includes IP addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamp, and referring/exit pages.
  • Local Storage: We use your browser's local storage to save your in-game preferences, sound settings, and high scores locally on your device.

2. Cookies and Web Beacons

Game Hub uses cookies to store information about visitors' preferences, to record user-specific information on which pages the user accesses or visits, and to personalize web page content based on visitors' browser type or other information.

3. Third-Party Games and Embedded Content

Our website features embedded games (such as via HTML5 iframes) hosted by third-party game providers. When you play these games, third-party content providers may collect data, set cookies, or monitor your interactions with the embedded content. We do not control these third-party websites and encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

4. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdSense)

We may display advertisements provided by third-party ad networks, including Google AdSense. These ad servers use technology (such as cookies and web beacons) to measure the effectiveness of their advertisements and personalize advertising content.

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on Game Hub.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.

5. Children's Information

Protecting children's privacy online is paramount. Game Hub does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child provided this kind of information on our website, please contact us immediately and we will promptly remove it.

6. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, please feel free to contact us via email at: support@gamehub.com.
