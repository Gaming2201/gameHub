About Game

Cannon Balls 3D is a casual physics-based arcade game where the objective is to destroy structures using a limited number of cannonballs. Players must aim strategically at weak spots, explosive barrels, or key support beams to cause complete structural collapses and clear levels efficiently.

• Physics-Based Destructibility: Structures disassemble and tumble dynamically depending on where and how hard your cannonballs hit.

• Strategic Aiming: Target critical points, TNT barrels, and support pillars to cause chain reactions and maximize damage.

• Ammo Management: Complete each level using the minimum amount of ammunition possible to earn higher star ratings.

• Progressive Difficulty: Features increasingly complex buildings, moving obstacles, and varied materials that require precise timing and planning.
