Garden Bloom

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

About Game

Garden Bloom is a colorful and relaxing Match-3 puzzle web/mobile game developed by Famobi. Set in a vibrant botanical world, the game follows the main character, Lucy, as she works to decorate and restore her dream garden by solving hundreds of flower-matching puzzles.

Game Overview

• Genre: Match-3 Puzzle / Casual

• Platforms: Web Browsers (HTML5), Android, iOS

• Developer: Famobi

• Main Character: Lucy

Key Features & Gameplay

• Classic Match-3 Mechanics: Swap and align 3 or more flowers of the same color to make them bloom, clear board obstacles, and complete level targets.

• Garden Decoration: Earn coins and stars by completing puzzle stages to unlock new plants, statues, and decorative items to customize Lucy's garden.

• Special Power-Ups: Match 4 or more flowers to create powerful boosters like Rainbow Bombs, Line Clears, and Lawnmowers to wipe out large clusters at once.

• Hundreds of Levels: Features over 2,000 unique levels with increasing difficulty, daily tasks, and special event challenges.

• Relaxing Experience: Designed with vibrant graphics, soothing background music, and a casual pace with no aggressive timers.
