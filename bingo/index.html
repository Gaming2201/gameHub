Game Rules:

  • Ships are placed horizontally or vertically.
  • No diagonal, overlapping, or adjacent ships.
  • Your board shows your ships and AI attacks.
  • 💥 Sound on hit, 💧 sound on miss, 💀 emoji when ship is sunk.
  • Background color shows whose turn it is.
  • Earn a "bump" every 10 turns to blast a 3x3 area.
